Spacing

  • Header padding: 20px
  • Entry card padding: 25px
  • Space between entry cards: 30px
  • Max content width: 700px
  • Rounded corners: 10px on cards, 8px on images
  • Line height: 1.6

Components

Entry card

White box with a 2px burgundy border and rounded corners. Each journal entry goes in one. This box is an entry card.

Quote

A quote goes here, in italics with an indented first line.

— Author Name

Image

Images are centered, up to 500px wide, with rounded corners. They lift up slightly when you hover over them.
